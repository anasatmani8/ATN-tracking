Delete your data

Kinset keeps your data only on your phone. To delete all of it:

  1. Open Kinset.
  2. Tap Profile on the Today screen.
  3. Tap Privacy and data.
  4. Tap Erase data on this device and confirm.

Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Nothing is kept by us because nothing is sent to us.
