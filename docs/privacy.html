What the app stores

Everything you enter (workouts, sets, plans, meals, recipes, water, sleep, body measurements, check-ins, notes and settings) is stored only in a database on your phone. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to any server.

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Camera

If you choose to scan a food barcode, the app uses the camera only on the scanning screen to read the barcode. No images are saved or sent anywhere. You can type the barcode instead.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data leaves your phone.

Food lookups

Barcodes are matched against foods you have saved on your phone. [Only if you enable online lookup: When a barcode is not found on your phone, only the barcode digits are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to find the product. No information about you is sent.]

AI coach

[Only if you enable the AI coach:] Suggestions on the Today and Coach screens are worked out on your phone. If you are signed in and agree on the consent screen, you can also chat with the coach: your messages, and summaries of your own logs that the coach looks up to answer (for example weekly training, food totals, water and sleep), are sent through our server to [Anthropic / OpenAI], which processes them to write the reply. We do not send your name or email; the provider receives a random-looking code instead. Our server stores no chat content, only how many messages you sent each day, to enforce limits. Your chat history stays on your phone; Profile → Withdraw chat consent deletes it. The coach never changes your data by itself: every change waits for you to tap Apply, and can be undone. The coach gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

Backups

On Android, the app's data is excluded from Google cloud backup. On iPhone, the app's data can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup of your phone, which you control. You can export all of your data at any time from Profile → Privacy and data → Export.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete everything, open Profile → Privacy and data → Erase data on this device, or uninstall the app. See Delete your data.

Health information

Kinset is a personal log, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Children

Kinset is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
