## Full description
Open the app, see what's planned today, and log it in seconds.

TRAIN
• Start today's workout in one tap, or an empty session
• Last time's numbers are filled in for you — tap once to save a set and start the rest timer
• Warm-up and working sets, dumbbell pairs and bodyweight, added or assisted load, time and distance
• Five editable starter plans: full body (2 or 3 days), upper/lower, push/pull/legs and home dumbbells
• Swap exercises for today only or change the plan; reschedule or skip days
• Personal bests, exercise history and load suggestions you choose to accept

EAT
• Meal diary with editable meal names, saved meals, recipes, favorites and copy from yesterday
• Plan the week's meals — they count only once you log them
• Energy and macros when known, with optional targets, or a meal-only view without numbers

RECOVER
• One-tap water with undo
• Sleep with bedtime, wake time and time awake; naps; time in bed vs time asleep kept distinct
• Optional body weight trend and measurements, visible only to you

REVIEW
• Weekly review: workouts done, records, how many days you logged, and one next step based on your data
• Optional weekly check-in for energy and soreness

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Works fully offline with no account
• Optional account to back up and sync between your devices
• Export everything as CSV or JSON
• No ads, no tracking

Kinset is a personal log, not medical advice.

## App Review notes (Apple) / testing instructions (Google)
- No account is needed; all features work offline as a guest.
- To see populated data quickly: on the last onboarding step tap "Explore with sample data" (or Profile → Explore with sample data). Exit from the banner on Today.
- If cloud sync is enabled in the build: demo account email/password provided separately in App Store Connect / Play Console. Account deletion: Profile → Account and sync → Delete account.
- Camera is requested only when the reviewer taps "Use camera" on the barcode screen; typing a code works without it.
- Notifications are requested only when a reminder is turned on (Recovery → Reminders).
