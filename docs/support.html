Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Everything works offline and stays on your phone.

How do I move my data to a new phone? Profile → Privacy and data → Export → Everything (JSON) and keep the file.

How do I change kg/lb or mL/fl oz? Profile → Units. Your saved entries are not changed, only how they are shown.

Reminders don't appear. Allow notifications for Kinset in your phone's Settings, and check the quiet hours in Recovery → Reminders.
